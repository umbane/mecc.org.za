## The MECC’s 3rd Meeting￼￼ (15 February 2023)
(Redirected from The MECC’s 3rd Meeting (15 February 2023))

PLACE : Muiz Kitchen, 150 Main rd Muizenberg

DATE : 15 February 2023

Present : David (ad hoc chairman), Chad, Joah, Kevin, Gareth.


Joah had to leave early unfortunately but he had done some research and sourced 18650 batteries at less than R30 each new, which works out near R2100 for a battery module. Ten of these in parallel should provide around 5Kw.

There is a draft Memorandum Of Understanding, (attached below) this is intended to be a living document that is it may be amended as needed.

We had a discussion and watched a video around Printed Circuit Boards (PCB), a bit of a trade off between buying in bulk and needing to proto-type.

We also discussed Battery Management Systems (BMS) which also has a similar trade off the the PCBs

Talked around batteries, from the video above, new batteries bought in bulk – from the same batch – should be mostly OK, 2nd hand batteries need to be tested and sorted into stocks of similar capacitance & resistance.

We also discussed around the idea of a specialised installed plug to connect directly into the DB board as well as the potential of a battery economy re-charging the batteries similar to re-filling gas bottles.

We also mentioned the need for a timelime/roadmap and milestones to keep ourselves on track.



## The MECC’s 4th Meeting￼￼ (20 February 2023)
(Redirected from The MECC’s 4th Meeting (20 February 2023))

PLACE : Muiz Kitchen, 150 Main rd Muizenberg

DATE : 20 February 2023

Present : David (ad hoc chairman), Chad, Joah, Gareth.


Started off with signing the MOU ,

We then moved on to a discussion around fundraising, we decided on a R50 per month membership fee and an initial R450 deposit for those wishing to engage in the battery build. We also mentioned the Muizenberg Market as a testing platform for a working prototype in order to raise interest as well as putting the word out more and converting those who showed interested on FaceBook into members.

We discussed a questionnaire similar to a FAQ in order to solidify our motives and methods, Chad has since drafted the questions which David has answered, this can be found here. Questionnaire

We also talked around forming a co-op and will access the relevant documents and if needed legal advice.

We then moved on to talk about the battery build specifically the battery holder PDB which David has ordered.

We also watched a video around a different type of battery build using a pre-punched nickle sheet to weld the batteries in place.

The boards David has ordered only cover the battery holder, we will still need to deal with the battery management system (BMS), the inverter, heat monitoring and dissipation etc.
