Our Cooperative History

mecc.org.za

#

Meeting on the Vlei

Initial founding meeting held on the Vlei, bringing together community members interested in creating a local electricity cooperative. This marked the beginning of our journey toward community-owned energy.

Jan 2023

Memorandum of Understanding Signed

Formal Memorandum of Understanding signed between founding members, establishing the framework and commitment for developing the Muizenberg Electricity Cooperative.

Feb 2023

Constitution Drafted

Official constitution drafted and adopted, establishing the cooperative's governance structure, principles, and operational framework based on the seven cooperative principles.

Mar 2023

First Battery Day Event

Inaugural Battery Day event held to educate the community about battery storage systems, renewable energy solutions, and their role in creating energy resilience for local communities.

Apr 2023

Energy Festival

Major community Energy Festival showcasing renewable energy technologies, cooperative principles, and sustainable solutions. This event significantly raised awareness and community engagement.

Jun 2023

Project in Hiatus

Cooperative activities entered a hiatus period for strategic planning, reflection on lessons learned, and preparation for the next phase of development and community engagement.

Dec 2023

Renewed Activity & Digital Platform

Resumption of cooperative activities with enhanced digital presence, website development, and renewed community outreach efforts. Focus on building sustainable membership and services.

2024

Future Development

Planned expansion of services, implementation of community energy projects, and development of innovative solutions like blockchain carbon credits and community microgrids.

2024+
